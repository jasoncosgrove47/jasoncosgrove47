# Jason Cosgrove, PhD

**Hybrid scientist × engineer.** I build agentic AI workflows for biomedical discovery, then validate them in the lab.

My goal is to build safe AI systems for diverse biomedical applications. I combine safety-critical systems
engineering, biology and computational biology to develop end-to-end discovery workflows.

🌐 **[jasoncosgrove47.github.io](https://jasoncosgrove47.github.io)** · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gH0WDeIAAAAJ&hl=en) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jason-cosgrove-18537a53/) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3121-2009) · Principal Scientist, Computational Medicine

### What I work on
- **Agentic discovery, validated at the bench:** proof-of-concept AI-agent workflows for early drug discovery, with prioritised targets taken to lab validation.
- **Evidence for decisions:** omics and literature evidence packages to evaluate targets and external assets.
- **Test-first, safety-minded engineering:** in my PhD I developed a test-driven approach to building multi-agent systems, using requirements-based acceptance tests and arguments that a model is fit for purpose.
- **Teaching scientists to use AI:** AI Training Lead for the British Society for Immunology.
